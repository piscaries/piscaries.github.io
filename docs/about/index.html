About

I'm Haifeng (Kevin) Zhao, an ML leader with 14+ years building ML and AI agent products for search, recommendations, ads ranking, e-commerce, legal, and go-to-market (GTM), including 6+ years managing teams in Silicon Valley. I have a PhD in CS/ML.

Many posts here were first published on Medium. I also build Nexus Digest, a daily briefing on AI news, papers and tools.
